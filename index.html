Fishes

Start as a baby fish. Eat, grow, and become the largest fish ever.

  • Hold your finger on the sea to swim toward it. (Arrow keys work on a computer.)
  • Hold 💨 Sprint to dash. It uses stamina and comes back when you stop.
  • Eat plankton and smaller fish. Bigger fish will eat you.
  • A red ring means a hunter has locked on to you. Swim away or grow bigger.
  • Reach size 100 on level 20 to win. You have 3 lives and 15 minutes.

Background items
More background items in the sea

None of these chase you. What they are:

  • Scenery, not food: clams in the sand, rocks on the bed, a rowing boat on the surface and a submarine drifting by. You swim past them.
  • Lion's mane jellyfish: big floating bells that sting you on contact (2 health). Not food.
  • Edible: shrimp, and squid (they jet off with a puff of ink, but you can catch them).
  • Sea urchins: spiny. Eating one costs you 2 size in spines. The purple one comes early; the long-spined one comes later.
  • Kelp forest: tall, thick seaweed. Small fish gather in it.

Off removes them at once. The fish, the difficulty and the trash setting do not change.
